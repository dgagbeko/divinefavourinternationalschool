Teachers' workshop with the Early Childhood Education Coordinator

On 8 October 2026, our teachers took part in a workshop at the school with Madam Georgina Nanewortor, Early Childhood Education Coordinator for the Ghana Education Service in Jasikan. The workshop is part of how we help our teachers deliver even better on our promises to our pupils, parents and partners.

Madam Nanewortor covered:

  • How a young child's brain works, and how to keep children calm, relaxed and free from stress
  • How to create an enabling environment for learning
  • Classroom management
  • What it takes for a school to be exceptional and unique
  • Time management, and using the new curriculum to meet the needs of every learner

We thank Madam Nanewortor for her time and guidance.
