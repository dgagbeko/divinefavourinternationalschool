Admissions

We admit children throughout the year when space allows. Here is how to enrol your child.

How to apply

  1. Visit or call the school

    Come to the school office or call [Phone number] to ask about space in your child’s class.

  2. Collect and fill the admission form

    Pick up a form from the office. Fill in your child’s details and the contacts of parents or guardians.

  3. Bring your child’s documents

    Return the completed form with:

    • A copy of the child’s birth certificate
    • [Number] recent passport photographs
    • The child’s health or weighing card

  4. Pay fees and start school

    Once fees are paid, we will give you the start date, uniform details and a list of items your child needs.
