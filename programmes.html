Programmes

Three levels that take children from their first days away from home to being ready for primary school.

  • Creche

    [Age range]

    A calm, homely room where our youngest children settle into school life with plenty of care and attention.

    • Regular routines for meals, rest and play
    • Songs, rhymes and picture books
    • Early speech, movement and sensory play

  • Nursery

    [Age range]

    Children start to learn in a more structured way while still learning mostly through play.

    • Colours, shapes, sounds and counting
    • Holding a pencil, drawing and early writing
    • Speaking and listening in English and [local language]

  • Kindergarten

    [Age range]

    Following Ghana’s kindergarten curriculum, children build the reading, writing and number skills they need for Primary 1.

    • Phonics, reading simple books and writing sentences
    • Numbers, addition, subtraction and measuring
    • Our world, creative arts and physical education
