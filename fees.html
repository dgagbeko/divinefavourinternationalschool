How to pay

  1. Dial *170# or open your MoMo app and choose Transfer Money, then MoMo User.
  2. Enter 024 967 0807 and check that the name shown is Elijah Tidola.
  3. Enter the amount and use your child's name and class as the reference.
  4. Send us the confirmation message on WhatsApp.

For fee amounts, call or WhatsApp us on 024 967 0807 or email divinefavourschoolsgh@gmail.com.
