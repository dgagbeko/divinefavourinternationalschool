About us

Our story, what we stand for, and the people who started Divine Favour.

About our school

Divine Favour International School was established in 2025 in Low-Cost, Jasikan, Oti Region, with a vision to provide quality, affordable education driven by innovative learning and modern teaching methods.

We believe every child is uniquely gifted and deserves an education and training that brings out their very best. Our promise is rooted in excellence, safety, character building, partnership, transparency, affordability and individual care. We are proud to serve families in Jasikan and the surrounding communities.

Our mission

To ignite a passion for lifelong learning through an innovative and accessible curriculum, empowering every student to achieve their full potential with confidence and joy.

Our vision

To be the centre of educational excellence, recognised globally for nurturing well-rounded students who lead and impact their communities.

Our core values

Excellence

We are committed to consistently achieving and surpassing high standards in academic, character and extracurricular pursuits, fostering a culture of distinction.

Faith

We cultivate an environment rooted in godly principles, encouraging resilience, compassion and a gracious outlook that guides our purpose and actions.

Integrity

We uphold unwavering honesty, strong moral principles and ethical conduct in all interactions, building trust and respect within our school community and beyond.

Hard work

We believe in the power of diligent effort, perseverance and dedication as essential pathways to personal growth and impact.

Our founders

Elijah Nchegnan Tidola

Co-Founder and Educational Leadership

Elijah is a co-founder of Divine Favour International School and a dedicated teacher at Bueman Senior High School. He is a PhD candidate in Education at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), and holds a Bachelor of Arts in Publishing Studies, a Diploma in Basic Education and an MPhil in Education. With over 16 years of teaching experience, he has shown a strong commitment to academic excellence and educational development. He is also a professional publisher and an accomplished author of several books in academic and Christian literature.

David Agbeko

Co-Founder and Strategic Development

David is a co-founder of Divine Favour International School. He holds a Master of Financial Insurance from the University of Toronto, Canada, and a Bachelor's degree in Actuarial Science from KNUST. He is a two-time scholar of the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program, at both bachelor's and graduate level. His experience spans financial services, institutional collaboration and education, with extensive work mentoring students at every level. David is an author and transformational leader committed to expanding access to education, scholarships and career opportunities.
