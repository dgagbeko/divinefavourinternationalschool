Welcome to Divine Favour

Established in 2025 in Low-Cost, Jasikan, we provide quality, affordable education driven by innovative learning and modern teaching methods.

We believe every child is uniquely gifted and deserves an education that brings out their very best, rooted in excellence, safety, character building and individual care.
