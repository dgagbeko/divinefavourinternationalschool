A day at Divine Favour

Every day mixes learning and play, so children grow in confidence as well as in letters and numbers.

Gentle care for the youngest

Babies and toddlers are held, fed, soothed and played with by carers who know them well.

Letters and first words

Phonics, songs and picture books build early reading.

Learning together

Small tables, plenty of books and a teacher close by.

Time to play and rest

Soft mats, games and quiet time when little bodies need it.

Songs and celebration

We sing, dance and cheer each other on.

Our classes

Crèche

Getting ready for school

Joining Divine Favour

Enrolling your child takes three simple steps.

  1. Visit us

    Come and see the classrooms, meet the teachers and ask anything you like.

  2. Fill in the admission form

    We'll give you the form and the list of fees and items your child will need.

  3. Start school

    Your child settles in gently, and we keep you updated every step of the way.
