What your child gains here

The first years of school shape how a child feels about learning. We keep classes warm, structured and full of things to do.

  • Early reading and numbers

    Letter sounds, first words, counting and simple sums, taught through songs, stories and hands-on play.

  • Play and creativity

    Drawing, music, building and outdoor games that build confidence and coordination.

  • Good character

    Children learn to share, take turns, say thank you and look after one another.

  • Safe and cared for

    Trained, attentive teachers and a clean, secure compound so parents can go to work with peace of mind.
